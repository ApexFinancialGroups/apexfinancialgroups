FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does the $750/month Managed Campaign include? +
It includes campaign strategy, ad creative, lead form setup, tracking, weekly reporting, and ongoing optimization. Any media budget stays in your control and can be discussed during the consultation.
How do the lead packs work? +
You can start with one lead for $50, or buy a discounted pack: 5 leads for $225, 15 leads for $600, or 35 leads for $1,225. Lead packs are a simple way to test quality before moving into monthly campaign management.
Are the leads exclusive? +
Yes. Leads from your campaign are routed to your agency only. We do not position this as a shared lead-list product.
Are the leads TCPA compliant? +
Our forms are built with consent language designed to support compliant follow-up. You should still follow your own compliance process and applicable federal and state rules when contacting prospects.
Do you require a minimum budget? +
No fixed budget is required on the site. During the free consultation, we can talk through the level of campaign activity that fits your market and goals before anything launches.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. Managed Campaign and Agency Partner plans are month to month. No long-term contract is required.
How do I get started? +
Request a free consultation. We will review your states, target client profile, follow-up process, and which plan makes the most sense for your agency. After that, we can send calendar times for a call.
